# 04 — Design Partner Outreach Kit

> Goal: 1 design partner = FREE 4-week pilot in exchange for feedback + testimonial.
> Do NOT pitch paid yet. The ask is tiny: "give me your competitor list, check one email
> per Friday."

---

## Who to target

Best first clients — people who ALREADY feel the pain:
- PMs at SaaS companies with 2–5 obvious competitors
- Founders who follow rivals on Twitter/X or RSS manually
- Marketing leads who compile "competitor updates" decks by hand

Where to find them (in order):
1. Your own LinkedIn connections — anyone at a SaaS company
2. r/ProductManagement, r/SaaS — careful, read self-promo rules first
3. Indie Hackers — post build-in-public update
4. Local/startup Slack & Discord communities you're already in

---

## Message A — Warm LinkedIn DM (use this first)

> Hey [Name] — quick one.
>
> I'm building a small service that sends a weekly email: **everything your competitors
> shipped that week** — their newsletters, product updates, announcements — condensed into
> a 3-minute Friday read.
>
> Before I charge anything, I'm giving it free to 3 companies for a month. You'd just send
> me the names of 3–5 competitors you care about; I handle the rest.
>
> Worth trying? If it saves you even one "wait, when did they launch THAT?" moment,
> it's done its job.

*Why it works: specific pain ("when did they launch THAT"), zero risk, tiny ask.*

---

## Message B — Cold but soft (LinkedIn/email to 2nd-degree)

> Hi [Name] — I noticed [Company] competes in [space] where things move fast.
>
> I run a weekly competitor-watch briefing: every Friday, a short summary of what each
> of your key rivals shipped — new features, pricing changes, announcements — pulled from
> their own newsletters and updates.
>
> I'm onboarding a few companies free this month while I refine the format. Want me to
> add [Company]? Just reply with 3–5 competitors you'd want tracked.

---

## Message C — Community post (Indie Hackers / relevant subreddit)

**Title:** I'll track your competitors and email you a weekly summary — free, looking for 3 test users

> Building a small tool that reads your competitors' newsletters/changelogs all week and
> emails you one digest every Friday: what shipped, what changed, with links.
>
> Looking for 3 people to test it free for a month. All I need:
> 1. Your top 3–5 competitors
> 2. An email to send the briefing to
> 3. Honest feedback at the end (would you pay? how much?)
>
> Comment or DM. No catch — I need real usage data before charging anyone.

---

## Handling replies

| Reply | Response |
|---|---|
| "Yes, interested" | "Great — just send me: 1) 3–5 competitor names (or their newsletter/blog URLs), 2) best email for the Friday briefing." |
| "How does it work?" | "I subscribe to their updates from my side, parse everything, and rank what shipped. You see only the output." |
| "What's the catch?" | "No catch. After a month I'll ask two questions: would you pay, and how much. That's my market research." |
| Silence after 4–5 days | One nudge: "Still happy to set you up if useful — otherwise no worries!" Then move on. |

---

## Onboarding checklist (per partner)

1. Get competitor sender addresses/newsletter URLs → subscribe from our account
2. Add rows to `Clients` tab: ClientID | Name | RecipientEmails | SenderAddress | BrandColor (their brand color = nice touch)
3. Confirm first Friday briefing arrives correctly
4. Week 2: ask "anything missing / anything noise?"
5. Week 4: THE two questions → testimonial request if positive

## Metrics to log during pilot

- Did they open/click? (add nothing fancy — just ask)
- Items per week per client (aim: 8–25; fewer = boring, more = noisy)
- Exact quotes about what they'd pay → this prices Phase 2
